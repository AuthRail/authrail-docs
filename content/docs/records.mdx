---
title: "Decisions, receipts and Activity Records"
description: "Read each record according to the claim it can support."
---

An Action joins several records because permission and execution answer different questions. Open the exact Action to see its deployment, mode, Operation Version, phase decisions, Rails, approvals and attempts. Keep historical signed records intact when rules or names later change.

| Record | What it explains | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision Certificate | The retained evaluation, exact versions and decision integrity | Provider completion or legal certification |
| Execution receipt | The authenticated executor's reported attempt or outcome | Independent provider verification |
| Approval and permit | Human review and permission for the exact bound call | That an effect started or completed |
| Activity Record | Linked customer-visible business activity | A replacement for the Action or evidence graph |
| Runtime log | Bounded operational diagnosis and failures | Durable business truth or proof of all interception |
| Internal telemetry | Correlated service performance and failure signals | Customer Evidence or authorization |

## Read permission and effect separately

For a denied Enforce call, inspect whether any start receipt exists and check the protected executor's effect ledger. For an allowed call, look for the reported attempt and outcome. A retained certificate with no receipt says nothing by itself about provider completion. An Observe would-deny result may accompany an actual execution because Observe is advisory.

The reference application's provider checks can read back an exact Stripe or QuickBooks record. That provider evidence is distinct from the receipt. Attestation verifies record integrity, not execution identity or compliance certification.

## Follow exact history

Certificates retain the exact evaluation rather than substituting the newest Rail. Receipt signatures use the retained executor assertion and public-key snapshot. Older receipts without those assertions remain identifiable as older evidence; never manufacture a signature for them.

Use linked Action and Activity Record references to move between authorization and the tenant's history. Respect tenant authorization and purpose-bound support access. Internal operational views do not grant staff unrestricted access to tenant payloads.

## Retain and export deliberately

The intended proof period, completeness, review and freshness come from the owning Test or Evidence requirement. Do not infer an export or retention guarantee that the installed deployment does not provide. Preserve the exact source references and [qualify Framework proof](/docs/framework-proof) before treating Runtime history as accepted Evidence.
